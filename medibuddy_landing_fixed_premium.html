OUR STORY

Built by a student
who understood the worry

Every day, millions of elderly people living alone in India miss their medication. Their children — working in different cities, caught up in busy lives — have no simple way to know if their parent is safe.

That constant worry inspired MediBuddy. A simple app that bridges the gap between elderly parents and their families — without making either of them do something complicated.

One reminder. One video. One tap. That is all it takes for a family to breathe easy.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
Total peace of mind.

1

Remind

At the right time every day, MediBuddy sends a loud, clear reminder to your elderly parent's phone. No confusion. No missing it.

2

Record

As your parent takes their medicine, the app records a short video as proof. No guessing. No doubt. It happened and it is saved.

3

Reassure

One tap and the whole family gets an instant notification. "Dad took his 8:30 AM medicine. All good." You can breathe now.
